Definitions for NSDUH, NHIS, and BRFSS

- Ecigaretter usage definition
  - NSDUH 2020-2022
    - VAPNICREC - Vaped nicotine or tobacco
      - 1: within past 30 days
      - 2: more than 30 days ago but within the past 12 months
      - 3: More than 12 months ago
    - VAPNICEVR - Ever Vaped nicotine or tobacco with e-cig or vaping device
      - 1: Yes
      - 2: No
  - NHIS 2019,2022
    - ecigstatus - identifies the current electronic cigarette or other electronic vaping product use status of sample adults. 
      - 1: Never
      - 2: Former
      - 3: Current 
  - BRFSS 2016-2018:  Current ecigarette users will be defined as those who report using them every day or some days
    - X_ECIGSTS
      - 1: Current use everyday
      - 2: Current use someday
      - 3: Former use
      - 4: Never use
    - ECIGNOW - Do you now use e-cigarettes/other vaping products every day, some days, or not at all?
      - 1: everyday
      - 2: someday
      - 3: not at all
    - ECIGARET - Have you ever used an e-cigarette or other electronic vaping product, even just one time, in your entire life?
      - 1: Yes
      - 2: No


  
- Smoking definition
  - NSDUH 2020-2022
    - CIG100LF - HAVE YOU SMOKED 100 CIGS IN YOUR LIFE
      - 1 or 3 or 5: Yes
    - cigyr - PAST YEAR USE
      - 0: Did not use in the past year
      - 1: Used within the past year
  - NHIS 2019,2022
    - SMOKESTATUS2 - this is a recoded variable indicating the respondent's current smoking status in categories of current smoker, every day current smoker, some day current       smoker, current smoker--unknown frequency of smoking, and also indicates former smoker, never smoked and "has smoked, current smoking status unknown."
      - 11: Current every day smoker
      - 12: Current some day smoker
  - BRFSS 2016-2018:  Current smokers will be defined as those who report using them every day or some days
    - SMOKER3 - Everyday smoker, Someday smoker, Former smoker, Non-smoker
      - 1: current, everyday
      - 2: current, somedays
      - 3: former
      - 4: never
    - RFSMOK3 - Adults who are current smokers
      - 1: No
      - 2: Yes
    - SMOKDAY2 - Do you now smoke cigarettes every day, some days, or not at all?
      - 1: Current Everyday
      - 2: current Somedays
      - 3: Not at all

  
- Depression definition
  - NSDUH 2020-2022
    - AMDEYR - identifies an adult as having MDE (Major Depressive Episode) in the prior year
      - 1: Yes
      - 2: No
    - AMDELT - identifies an adult as having a major depressive episode (MDE) in their lifetime
      - 1: Yes
      - 2: No 
  - NHIS 2019,2022 - Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by:
    - phqintr - little interest or pleasure in doing things?
    - phqdep - feeling down, depressed, or hopeless?
    - phqsleep - trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much? 
    - phqengy - feeling tired or having little energy?
    - phqeat - poor appetite or overeating?
    - phqbad - feeling bad about yourself, or that you are a failure, or have let yourself or your family down? 
    - phqconc - trouble concentrating on things, such as reading the newspaper or watching television?
    - phqmove - moving or speaking so slowly that other people could have noticed? Or the opposite, being so
      fidgety or restless that you have been moving around a lot more than usual?
      - 1: Several Days
      - 2: More than half the days
      - 3: Nearly everyday
    - PHQ-9 Score ranges from 0-24. None= 0-5. Mild = 5-9. Moderate = 10-14. Severe = 15. 
      - phq_score = phqintr+phqdep+phqsleep+phqengy+phqeat+phqbad+phqconc+phqmove
  - BRFSS 2016-2018 
    - MENTHLTH - Now thinking about your mental health, which includes stress, depression, and problems with emotions, for how many days during the past 30 days was your mental health not good?
      - 1-30: Number of days
    - ADPLEAS1 - Over the last 2 weeks, how often been bothered by having little interest/pleasure in doing things. Would you say this happens
      - 1: Never
      - 2: For several days
      - 3: For more than half the days
      - 4: Nearly everyday
    - ADDOWN1 - Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by feeling down, depressed or hopeless? Would you say this happens
      - 1: Never
      - 2: For several days
      - 3: For more than half the days
      - 4: Nearly everyday
    - PHQ2 (created variable score from 0 to 6) = ADPLEASE1-1+ADDOWN1-1
    - ADDEPEV2 - (Ever told) you that you have a depressive disorder, including depression, major depression, dysthymia, or minor depression
      - 1: Yes
      - 2: No
